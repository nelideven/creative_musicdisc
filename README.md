# Creative Music Disc

A Fabric mod that adds C418's Creative Mode background music as playable music discs in Minecraft!

## What's added?
- Music Disc: **C418 - Aria Math**
- Music Disc: **C418 - Biome Fest**
- Music Disc: **C418 - Blind Spots**
- Music Disc: **C418 - Dreiton**
- Music Disc: **C418 - Haunt Muskie**
- Music Disc: **C418 - Taswell**

## Features
- Accessible via the **Tools & Utilities** creative tab.
- Integrated into vanilla chest loot tables (Spawners, Dungeons, Mineshafts, etc).
